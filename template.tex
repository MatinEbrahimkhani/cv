\section{Publications and Conferences}

%------------------------------------------------
\textbf{PerSpaCor: Correcting Space and ZWNJ Errors in Persian Text with Transformer Models} \\
\textit{Matin Ebrahimkhani, Ebrahim Ansari} \\
\textit{Proceedings of the 15th International Conference on Recent Advances in Natural Language Processing (RANLP 2025)} \\
Developed and evaluated BERT-based sequence labeling models for correcting Persian spacing and ZWNJ errors. Trained on over 12.7M annotated words from Bijankhan and Peykare corpora, achieving a macro-average F1-score of 97.26\% (98.38\% with user feedback). Proposed novel classifier architectures, advancing Persian NLP and demonstrating state-of-the-art performance on this fundamental text normalization task.
